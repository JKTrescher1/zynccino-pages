Common questions

My settings aren't syncing across devices.
Settings sync requires being signed in with the same account on both devices. Check Settings → Account to confirm you're signed in.

How do I reset my password?
From the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot password" and follow the emailed link.

How do I delete my account and data?
Email us at the address above and we'll delete your account and associated data. See our Privacy Policy for details on what data is stored.

The app crashed or something looks broken.
Please email us with your device model, OS version, and a description of what happened — screenshots help a lot.
