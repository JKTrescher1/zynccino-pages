Privacy Policy

Zynccino · Last updated September 21, 2026

Zynccino ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal tracker for nicotine pouch use. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what stays on your device.

Account information

Creating an account is optional but required to sync your settings across devices. You can sign up with an email address and password, or with Google Sign-In. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive the name and email address associated with your Google account. Account authentication is handled by our database provider, Supabase — we don't run our own authentication servers.

Data stored in your account

Once signed in, the following settings sync to our database so they're available across your devices:

  • Your regional price estimate and pouches-per-can setting
  • Your baseline daily usage estimate
  • Your onboarding responses (habit rating and quitting intent)
  • Your savings goal name and target price

Data kept only on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Your log of pouch use and "skip" events
  • Your active quit challenge and challenge history
  • Forum posts, comments, and votes (the forum is currently a local, single-device feature — content isn't shared with other users)

If you delete the app, this locally-stored data is deleted with it.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled directly on your device using your operating system's local notification system. We do not use push notifications, and we have no record of when or whether a notification is shown to you.

What we don't do

  • We don't use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't access your device's camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

Age requirement

Zynccino is intended for adults who already use nicotine pouch products and is not directed at children. You must meet the legal minimum age for nicotine product purchase and use in your jurisdiction to use this app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
